About BrixClub

BrixClub Jewelry started at local markets — small Lego earrings, bracelets, and buildable bits that make people stop and smile. Now we're taking it online so you can get your brix without waiting for a market day.

Every piece is hand-built. That means no two orders are ever exactly the same — and that's the whole point.
